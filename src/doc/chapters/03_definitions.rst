Definitions
===========

Terms defined elsewhere
-----------------------

The following terms are used in this Technical Report as defined in the cited
documents.

3.1.1 agent
    [b-ISO/IEC 22989] An automated entity that senses and responds to its environment and takes actions to achieve its goals.

3.1.2 artificial intelligence system
    [b-ISO/IEC 22989] An engineered system that generates outputs such as content, forecasts, recommendations or decisions for a given set of human-defined objectives.

3.1.3 risk
    [b-ISO/IEC 23894] The effect of uncertainty on objectives, where an effect is a deviation from the expected and can be positive or negative.

3.1.4 human oversight
    [b-EU AI Act] Measures enabling natural persons to monitor, interpret and intervene in the operation of an AI system, commensurate with its risks, level of autonomy and context of use; the same instrument distinguishes human-in-the-loop, human-on-the-loop and human-out-of-the-loop configurations.

3.1.5 prompt injection
    [b-OWASP LLM Top 10] An attack in which crafted input causes a model to disregard its instructions, leak data or produce harmful output.

3.1.6 human-AI configuration
    [b-NIST AI 600-1] Risks arising from the design and operation of interactions between humans and AI, including over-reliance, emotional entanglement and anthropomorphisation.

3.1.7 human dignity and well-being
    [b-UNESCO AI Ethics] The first-order ethical commitments that the Recommendation on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence requires AI systems to respect.

Terms defined in this Technical Report
--------------------------------------

This Technical Report does not define any new terms. Appendix I provides a set
