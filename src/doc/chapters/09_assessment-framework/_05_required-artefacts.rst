Required artefacts
""""""""""""""""""

The framework requires four artefacts:

-  a risk taxonomy (enumerating human-facing risks: emotional dependence,
   sycophancy-induced judgement erosion, reality drift, role/boundary violation,
   isolation reinforcement, compulsive use);

-  a risk-signal schema (the structured observables and their confidence);

-  an intervention policy (the graduated response rules);

-  an evidence record (the auditable history of signals, interventions, outcomes
   and revisions).
