Roles and accountability
""""""""""""""""""""""""

The provider is accountable for the design, deployment and monitoring of the
guardrails. The user has autonomy rights, including the right to understand what
monitoring occurs and the right to exit. A designated safety function within the
provider owns the intervention policy and reviews the evidence. External
auditors or regulators may access the evidence record under defined conditions.
Where the system addresses minors or vulnerable users, the review body sits
outside the product organisation.
