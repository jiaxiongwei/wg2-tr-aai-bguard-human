Adapting the framework across cultures, ethics, religions and regulation
""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""

This clause summarises how the assessment framework adapts across cultures,
ethics, religions and regulation. The framework is not a single global rule set:
it is a common minimum that is parameterised per jurisdiction and per cultural
context.

Adaptation draws on the eleven dimensions in Appendix II, whose entries describe
what varies and why, and on the assessment factors in 8.2, which are the
observable quantities to be collected. The technical description of the
dimensions is given in Appendix II and that of the localisation mechanism in
Appendix IV. Specific cases and events are recorded in Appendix IV.

The two values the framework holds constant are transparency and the prohibition
of exploiting vulnerability; everything else is a documented parameter rather
than an assumption.
